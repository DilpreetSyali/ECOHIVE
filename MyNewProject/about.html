About ECOHIVE

Welcome to ECOHIVE! At ECOHIVE, we're redefining waste management and recycling with smart, sustainable technology. Discover our solutions that help keep urban areas clean while rewarding eco-friendly choices.

Smart Sensor-Operated Waste Bins: We’re installing sensor-equipped bins in public areas that detect fill levels in real-time. As these bins near capacity, our system is automatically alerted, enabling timely pickups to prevent overflow and maintain cleanliness in public spaces.

Dynamic Pickup Scheduling Algorithm: Our intelligent scheduling algorithm optimizes waste collection by analyzing fill levels, locations, and historical data from sensor-equipped bins. This approach minimizes travel distances and fuel usage, reducing overall emissions and improving efficiency.

Revolutionizing Recycling with Technology: Our Point Redemption and Reward System transforms recycling into a rewarding habit, promoting sustainability by incentivizing users with valuable points they can redeem for rewards.
